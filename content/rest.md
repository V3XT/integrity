Characters can spend time resting to remove various [[condition|conditions]]. The amount of [[time]] needed is specified by the condition. Resting requires a reasonable expectation of safety. 

When resting for a [[time#Turn]], it's reasonable to rest almost anywhere as long as there are no known threats nearby.

When resting for a proper [[time#Watch|Watch]], characters will want to be somewhere secure. Preferably in the safety of a town, but a secluded room in a dungeon can make do.
