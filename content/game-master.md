The Game Master is the player responsible for adjudicating the the results of a [[test]]. They control the setting.

# Duties

**Give players the benefit of the doubt** -  You are the window into the world for the players. It can be easy for them to misunderstand something. Be transparent about the expected results of their actions, and inform them of the difficulty of actions they'd take.
