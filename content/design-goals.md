---
title: Design Goals
---
# Simple Enough for Mom

The game should simple enough to to teach to anyone with a desire to play. It shouldn't require familiarity with other RPGs.

# Meaningful and Interesting Choices

At all stages of play, players should be making choices. A choice is only meaningful if it has consequences, and it is only interesting if their is a discernible difference between the options.


> [!Info] 
> Choosing to go *left* or *right* in a dungeon is not an interesting choice, even if one route leads to reward and the other peril, unless the difference has been foreshadowed somehow.

# Quick Resolution

The narrative action should not be restrained by the rules. The pace of the game should be able to handle differing timeframes. The mechanics of a situation should never be more complicated than narrative.

# Diegetic Character Growth

Progression and purpose should be found narratively through actions taken by the players. 


# Playable Without Digital Tools

The game should be easily playable without the requirement of digital tools. It's ok to have digital tools, but they shouldn't be necessary.
