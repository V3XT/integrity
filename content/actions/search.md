---
title: Search
---
Searching is a common action performed when characters [[exploration|Explore]] that reveals hidden information within an area. When exploring in turns, searching should be a discussion between the players and the GM.

Searching typically doesn't require a [[test]], it simply takes a set amount of time. Some situations may be a good case for [[test#Extended Tests]], such as searching for a specific book in an abandoned library. In that case, it will normally be a Will test.
