---
title: Difficult Terrain
aliases:
  - Difficult Terrain
---
When [[movement|moving]], you lose 1 die for each zone with difficult terrain that you cross.

> [!faq]
> If the area you're leaving, or the area you're going to is difficult, you lose 1 die. If they're both difficult, you lose 2 dice.

> [!Example]
> An area can be difficult terrain for many reasons. These are just a few options.
> - A steep roof
> - An icy lake
> - Sticky mud
> - Piles of rubbish
