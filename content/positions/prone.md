---
title: Prone
aliases:
  - Prone
---
Prone characters lose 1 die from all relevant [[test|Tests]].

The difficulty to hit a prone character with a [[ranged-attack|ranged attack]] is increased by one.

The difficulty to hit a prone character with a [[melee-attack|melee attack]] is decreased by one.

After a character completes an action, they *may* stand up and stop being prone. They may also choose to fall prone at the end of any action.
