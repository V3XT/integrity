---
title: Prone
---
Prone characters lose 1 die when making an attack, or [[movement|moving]].

The difficulty to hit a prone character with a ranged attack is increased by one.

The difficulty to hit a prone character with a melee attacks is decreased by one.

After a character completes an action, they *may* stand up and stop being prone. They may also choose to fall prone at the end of any action.
