Characters represent anything in the fiction that's able to make decisions that affect the world. This includes both the player's characters, and any NPCs controlled by the GM.

Character's have three primary attributes, Knack, Might, and Will. They're rated from -5 to +5. Whenever a character makes a [[test|test]], they will add or remove a number of dice based on the attribute being tested.
