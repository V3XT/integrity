---
title: Exploration
---


During [[time#Turns|Turns]] if characters are thoroughly searching each room, every zone will take 10 minutes. If they are moving without searching they can either move cautiously at a rate of 4 zones per turn, or recklessly at a rate of 20 zones per turn. While moving cautiously, characters are less likely to be detected, and will automatically notice threats that aren't disguised. While moving recklessly, characters will automatically trigger traps they aren't aware of, and are likely to be detected. Moving recklessly should typically only be done during dire circumstances, or in areas that are known to be safe.
